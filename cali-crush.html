Cali Crush

A separate partner-sourced indoor flower range with California-derived genetics.

Partner-sourced range

California-derived genetics, grown indoors by a supply partner.

Cali Crush is a menu of indoor flower from a partner grower, built around California-derived cultivars selected for terpene expression, bag appeal and consistent buyer demand.

This range is separate from the group's cultivation and acquisition programme. It is sourced from a partner, and it does not make its country of origin a market in that programme.

What to know

  • Indoor and light-assist flower from a partner grower
  • Five cultivars in rotation across sativa, balanced and indica leans
  • Certificates, lab results and batch documents are provided per batch where available
  • Suitability for any market depends on that market's licensing and import rules

How supply works

Documentation

Certificates, testing and batch documents are shared per batch where available. We do not make a blanket statement of certification for the range.

Destinations

Whether a batch can be supplied to a given country depends on that country's licensing, import and medical or adult-use rules. Destinations are confirmed case by case.

Pricing & freight

Pricing depends on volume and product selection. Freight and handling are agreed per order.
