Fixer

A high-CBD, low-THC craft cultivar — available as hand-graded flower and as seed.

Flower & Seed

CBD-forward, low-THC, with results tested batch by batch.

Fixer is a high-CBD, low-THC cultivar — a dense, resinous flower with a rich CBD profile. In the two batches tested independently, Δ09-THC was not detected and total THC stayed under 0.7%, alongside a spread of minor cannabinoids.

It's offered two ways: as hand-graded craft flower, and as seed for growers who want to bring the cultivar into their own programme.

Why buyers stock it

  • High-CBD, low-THC — CBD up to ~17%, Δ09-THC non-detect on the tested batches
  • Minor cannabinoids present: CBG, CBDV, CBDVA
  • Independently lab-tested — batch certificates available on request
  • Available as craft flower and as seed
