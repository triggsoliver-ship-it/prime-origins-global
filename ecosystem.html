The operating approach

Connect the operation. Keep the record.

Our approach brings cultivation and processing together with documented product quality, commercial workflows and settlement. The aim is a clear record from origin through to the customer.

Cultivation and quality

Apply the right growing methods and product standards in each market.

Processing and distribution

Coordinate local production and supply through the appropriate licensed channels.

Provenance and commerce

Connect batch records, supporting documents and trading workflows through Prime Origins.

Settlement

Support eligible business payments through Greenback's digital-dollar infrastructure.
