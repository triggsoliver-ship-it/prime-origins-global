Origin markets

A growing network of origin markets.

Each market and product is described separately. Nothing here implies an open trade route between the places shown.

California · Cultivation

Vista Ranch, Anderson Valley

  • 60,000 sq ft licensed canopy — full-term outdoor cultivation
  • Grown in the Navarro River Cannabis region
  • Regenerative methods: smart pots, engineered soil, watershed restoration
  • First harvest projected at 2,094 lbs bud and 837 lbs trim — a projection, not completed production
Morocco · Supply relationship

Traditional Moroccan hash

  • Supply relationships with partner farms operating inside Morocco's regulated framework (ANRAC)
  • Traditional hash from a long-established hash origin
  • Chain-of-custody documentation — COAs, batch records, export files

Further markets. We are exploring additional origin markets. Ownership, operating status and partnership terms are published only once they are documented. A cultivation licence in one jurisdiction does not authorise trade into another; every product and route is subject to its own licences and approvals.

Operations spotlight

Stewards of the Navarro River terroir

Calnejo — our cultivation brand at Vista Ranch — sits high above the Anderson Valley, where redwood-cooled air, full-term sun and living soil produce cannabis that tastes of its place. Our cultivators helped write California's appellation rules — and they farm to them every day.

No shortcuts, no synthetics. Smart pots, engineered soil and watershed restoration keep the land healthier than we found it, season after season.
