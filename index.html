Our markets

Distinct markets. One operating approach.

Our acquisition and development programme connects cultivation, processing, product quality and commercial infrastructure. Each market has a clear role, and each project progresses through its own approvals and completion milestones.

United States · State

New York

Cultivation, processing and distribution
Programme: Acquisition and integration

New York is central to our US operating strategy. We are developing an integrated approach to cultivation, processing and wholesale distribution, with a focus on coordinated production and documented supply to licensed retail partners.

Focus: Cultivation · Processing · Wholesale distribution
United States · State

California

Cultivation, craft and provenance
Programme: Acquisition programme

Calnejo Estates at Vista Ranch in Anderson Valley forms the basis of our California cultivation strategy. Its Mendocino heritage, sun-grown approach and focus on product quality inform the methods and provenance standards we aim to share across the group.

Project: Calnejo Estates — Anderson Valley, Mendocino County
Europe · Country

North Macedonia

European medical-cannabis pathway
Programme: Target selection and development

Our North Macedonia strategy is to identify and acquire a licensed medical-cannabis producer, then develop its production and EU-GMP capability for permitted European medical-market supply. Germany is a target market, with site selection, certification and export approvals forming part of the development process.

Project: Licensed-producer opportunity — target selection under way
Africa · Country

Lesotho

Hemp and CBD partner development
Programme: Partner development and proposed acquisition

Mountain Breeze is the focus of our Lesotho hemp and CBD programme. Building on a sales and distribution relationship, the proposed development combines local production potential with cultivation expertise, genetics and technical support. Acquisition and expansion remain subject to site, licence and stock verification.

Project: Mountain Breeze — non-intoxicating hemp and CBD
